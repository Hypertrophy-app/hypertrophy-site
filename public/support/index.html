Help

Support

Hypertrophy is still in testing. Reports are useful, and the detail you include makes them much faster to act on.

Contact

Email kaadz@protonmail.com. For a bug, include:

  • Your device model and Android version
  • The app version, from the build name or version code
  • What you did, what you expected, and what happened
  • Logcat output if you can capture it, with personal data removed

Public issues and ideas

Defects and scoped proposals are tracked in the issue tracker. Questions and open-ended ideas belong in Discussions.

Please do not include real training data, account details, or keys in a public issue.

Security

If you find a security problem, do not open a public issue. Use private vulnerability reporting. The full policy is in the repository's SECURITY.md.

Data requests

Access, correction, and deletion requests can be sent to the same email address. See the privacy policy for what is stored and what stays on your device.
