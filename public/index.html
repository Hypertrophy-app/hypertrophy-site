Strength training that works without a signal.

Hypertrophy logs every set on your phone, coaches your form on the device, and syncs to your squad only when you choose. No connection in the gym is not a problem.

01

Offline by default

The screen reads from local storage, never from a server. Plan a week, log your sets and track personal records in a dead zone. Finished sessions leave through an outbox when you are back online.

02

A coach that stays on your phone

Pose estimation runs locally. Reps, range of motion and tempo are tracked in memory, form faults are flagged, and the camera frame is discarded immediately. No video is recorded or uploaded.

03

Squads without the noise

Share a six-character invite code, see who is training now, send a finished session to the feed and talk in group chat. Stealth mode hides your session while you stay in the room.

04

Progress you can verify

A recovery map for every muscle group, a 90-day consistency heatmap, a trophy wall for personal records and estimated one-rep-max progression over time. Export sessions to Health Connect when you want them there.

Hypertrophy is in testing ahead of a Play Store release. Nothing to install yet, and no email list to join.
