Privacy Policy

Draft pending review. This policy has not taken effect. It describes the shipped design of an app still in testing and must be checked by a qualified reviewer and re-verified against the code before the Play Store submission. Remove this notice when approved.

Hypertrophy writes your training log to your phone first. Plans, templates, personal records, and recaps stay there. A completed session also syncs to your private account so your history survives a lost device. This policy explains what stays on the device, what leaves it, and what never does.

1. What stays on your device

Your workout plans, saved templates, weekly schedule, personal records, session recaps, dashboard layout, and squad cache are written to a local database on your phone. The app reads from that database, so planning a week and logging a set do not depend on a connection.

Local rows are keyed to your Clerk user id. Signing out leaves them on disk, but a different account cannot read them. Deleting the app or clearing its data removes them.

Finished sessions are queued in a local outbox and uploaded in the background when a connection is available. A failed upload leaves the session on your phone and retries later; it is not discarded.

2. Account and identity

You need an account to use Hypertrophy. Identity is handled by Clerk, which supports an email code, Google, or a passkey. Clerk stores your email address and the credentials that keep you signed in on this device. Hypertrophy never receives or stores your password.

Signed-in requests carry a short-lived Clerk token. Our backend verifies that token and stores your Clerk user id with the data described below. It does not store your email address.

3. What leaves the device

When you are signed in, a completed session is uploaded automatically. Workout plans, templates, weekly schedules, personal records, and recaps are not uploaded. The payload contains:

CategoryExamplesWhy
AccountClerk user idTying synced rows to your account
TrainingSession title, start and end time, and each set's exercise id, reps, weight, range of motion, tempo label, and form scoreKeeping your history available after a reinstall
SquadSquad name, invite code, membership, presence status, chat messagesRunning the squad and showing chat

All traffic to the backend uses HTTPS, and squad chat uses an encrypted WebSocket. We do not sell data, share it with advertisers, or send it to analytics providers.

4. Health Connect

Health Connect access is optional and user initiated. If you export a session, Hypertrophy writes a workout record to Health Connect on the device. The app requests write access only and does not read your health data. You can revoke that permission in system settings at any time.

5. Camera and form coaching

The form coach uses the camera and on-device pose estimation. Video frames are read into memory, analyzed, and closed immediately. No frame, recording, or raw landmark stream is saved to the device or sent over the network. Only compact training metrics, such as a rep count or a form score, become part of the session record. Camera frames never leave the phone.

6. What we do not collect

  • No precise or background location
  • No contacts, call logs, SMS, or calendar
  • No photos, videos, or files from your device
  • No Health Connect data uploaded to our backend
  • No advertising identifier or device fingerprint
  • No advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs
  • No payment information. The app has no subscriptions or in-app purchases.

7. Retention

Local data remains on your device until you delete the app or clear its data. Synced sessions, squad membership, and chat remain on our backend while your account exists, so your history is available after a reinstall. Squad messages remain for the life of the squad.

8. Your choices and deletion

You can revoke Health Connect and camera permissions in Android settings at any time, leave a squad, and sign out. Signing out stops sync and hides local rows from the next account, but it does not delete them from the phone or from our backend.

An account and data deletion flow is being prepared before launch. When it ships it will remove your Clerk user record, synced training history, squad membership, and squad chat state. Until then, write to the contact address below and we will delete your data manually. Uninstalling the app removes only the copy on your phone.

9. Children

Hypertrophy is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

10. Security

Data in transit is encrypted. Backend credentials, Clerk secrets, and release signing keys are held in Cloudflare and GitHub secrets, never in the app or its source repositories. No system is perfectly secure, and we will notify affected users if a breach affects their data.

11. Changes

We will update this page when the app's data handling changes and update the effective date below. Material changes will be called out in the release notes.
